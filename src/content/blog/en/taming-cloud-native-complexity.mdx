## The trap

Everyone wants microservices. Few ask: *what problem am I actually solving?*

In my experience, the default should always be a **well-structured monolith**. Extract services only when you have clear evidence of a bounded context, a scaling constraint, or a team boundary.

## Observable by default

Whether monolith or microservice, one principle is non-negotiable: **structured, request-scoped logging from day one**.

```json
{"ts":"2026-08-02T10:00:00Z","level":"info","trace_id":"abc123","span":"handleOrder","duration_ms":342,"db_queries":4}
```

Without trace IDs, you're debugging in the dark. Start with OpenTelemetry instrumentation before you write your second endpoint.
